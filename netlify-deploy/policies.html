Shipping, Returns & Privacy

Use this page as your public policy. Update delivery times, cities, and return rules before launch.

Shipping

Orders are confirmed by phone or WhatsApp before delivery. Delivery price and timing depend on the city and courier availability.

Returns & Exchanges

Customers can request an exchange for size or defect after receiving the order. Items must be unused and in original condition.

Payment

Cash/WhatsApp is available. Bank-card payment works only through a secure hosted gateway link from your bank or payment provider.

Privacy

Customer name, phone, address, order items, and review photos are used only to process orders, delivery, support, and store reviews.
